# UX-10 — Organize Creative identity into focused editing sections

**Status:** In progress · **Priority:** P1  
**Feature:** [Consistent interface and editorial experience](../features/ui-ux-consistency.md)  
**Dependencies:** [UX-01](UX-01.md), [UX-02](UX-02.md), [UX-03](UX-03.md), [UX-04](UX-04.md), [UX-05](UX-05.md)

As an editor, I want to edit one part of the brand at a time and see what will be saved, so the creative profile is understandable.

## Current evidence

Creative identity's contextual tabs open and scroll to Group accordions within a long form. Strategy, place fidelity, voice, references, and numbering coexist, with Save creative profile at the bottom.

**Surfaces:** [creative-profile-panel.tsx](../../src/app/creative-profile-panel.tsx) · [creative-profile-fields.tsx](../../src/app/creative-profile-fields.tsx) · [radar-dashboard.tsx](../../src/app/radar-dashboard.tsx).

## Acceptance criteria

1. Make Profile, Voice, Visual, and Assets coherent sections with persistent section navigation and one visible save scope. Do not style scroll anchors as if they isolate content when all sections remain visible.
2. Place identity and language under Profile, voice guidance under Voice, palette/logo/composition/place policy under Visual, and references/characters under Assets. Keep strategy links explicit when fields remain owned by the creative profile.
3. Show a compact summary of effective settings and the impact on future work. Preserve historical snapshots and distinguish changes that invalidate current approval from presentation-only edits.
4. Give voice ranges descriptive endpoint labels and numeric values. Use helpful defaults and concise explanations instead of unexplained dimensions.
5. Keep Save accessible during long edits and distinguish independently persisted uploads/reference changes from profile changes.
6. Expose maps diagnostics and provider configuration through an advanced section, with current policy and required evidence still visible.

## Validation

Change voice, switch sections, update a visual rule, and save once. Verify all pending values persist, existing saved references keep their own state, and historical drafts retain their snapshots.

## Scope and delivery

This reorganizes current data ownership; it does not copy strategy or brand fields into Editorial Lines.

## Implementation progress — September 28, 2026

- Creative Profile has a sticky Save/Cancel row, a visible unsaved state, and a browser reload warning for pending edits. Focused list fields update the saved draft while typing.
- Remaining: reorganize Profile, Voice, Visual, and Assets into focused sections and review the full edit/preview journey on mobile and desktop.

Apply the feature's [shared visual contract and definition of done](../features/ui-ux-consistency.md), including UXDSL, English UI copy, responsive/keyboard checks, and preservation of approvals and history. Record implementation and visual evidence before changing status. The [component audit](../ui-ux-component-audit.md) lists every affected consumer.
