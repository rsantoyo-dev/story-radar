# UX-06 — Make Today a clear preparation and decision workspace

**Status:** In progress · **Priority:** P0  
**Feature:** [Consistent interface and editorial experience](../features/ui-ux-consistency.md)  
**Dependencies:** [UX-01](UX-01.md), [UX-04](UX-04.md), [UX-05](UX-05.md)

As an editor starting the day, I want to see the next decision and resume work immediately, so I spend less time interpreting the dashboard.

## Current evidence

Today combines Overview, preparation, and recommendations. Attention is a total with generic navigation; production metrics include 'Ready / published'. Preparation and planner use separate time-zone controls and stage language.

**Surfaces:** [topic-overview-panel.tsx](../../src/app/topic-overview-panel.tsx) · [daily-preparation-panel.tsx](../../src/app/daily-preparation-panel.tsx) · [daily-editorial-planner-panel.tsx](../../src/app/daily-editorial-planner-panel.tsx) · [daily-preparation.types.ts](../../src/app/modules/stories/daily-preparation.types.ts).

## Acceptance criteria

1. Place actionable pending work first, daily preparation next, and compact operational summaries after it. A pending item identifies Story, version, reason, and next action.
2. Reuse OVW-03/04 data contracts for exact attention and production links. If those records are missing, identify the limited summary honestly rather than inventing item counts or links.
3. Show the preparation line, time zone, target stage, completed results, and the next human checkpoint before running. Starting and continuing are explicit; completed stages open their results.
4. Label recommendation approval 'Approve Story for production'. Keep Story, script, image, and publication decisions distinct. Preserve the current server check that requires human approval of the exact draft before images.
5. Show one primary recommendation with its reason and expandable alternatives, deferrals, and uncertainty. A stale recommendation remains visibly stale.
6. Clarify browser-dependent versus durable operations from their actual service contract; keep available results reachable on return and offer recovery from the failed step.

## Validation

Use first-day, no-candidates, stale recommendation, completed preparation, approval-needed, and failed-step fixtures. An editor should find a recorded pending review in one action from Today when OVW data supports it.

## Scope and delivery

Depends on existing OVW-03/04 capabilities for detailed queues. Do not add a workflow engine or automatically approve a draft to simplify the screen.
