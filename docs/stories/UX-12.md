# UX-12 — Clarify editorial strategy, inherited settings, and time periods

**Status:** In progress · **Priority:** P1  
**Feature:** [Consistent interface and editorial experience](../features/ui-ux-consistency.md)  
**Dependencies:** [UX-01](UX-01.md), [UX-02](UX-02.md), [UX-03](UX-03.md), [UX-04](UX-04.md), [UX-05](UX-05.md)

As an editor, I want to understand what my strategy affects and which settings a line inherits, so I can configure discovery and evaluation reliably.

## Current evidence

Editorial lines use a row of buttons and raw ISO ranges, IANA time-zone text, and comma-separated fields. Acquisition lenses expose internal keys and a 'publish' operation. Profile copy implies one priority score, while the product preserves separate editorial and growth signals.

**Surfaces:** [editorial-profile-panel.tsx](../../src/app/editorial-profile-panel.tsx) · [editorial-lines-panel.tsx](../../src/app/editorial-lines-panel.tsx) · [acquisition-lenses-panel.tsx](../../src/app/acquisition-lenses-panel.tsx) · [radar-dashboard.tsx](../../src/app/radar-dashboard.tsx).

## Acceptance criteria

1. Use Criteria, Editorial lines, and Angles as clear destinations with a compact summary and scoped edit form. Place keyword preferences with related criteria.
2. Show Topic defaults, line inheritance, and explicit overrides separately. Show the effective sources and research settings before a collection run.
3. Replace raw time-range entry with labeled date/time controls and a readable time-zone selector, retaining exact offsets and a supported custom-value path.
4. Give numeric weights and limits field-level validation and explain their actual purpose. Preserve separate editorial and growth meanings and current compatibility defaults.
5. Expose acquisition labels, definitions, fallback, and target share before technical keys. Use an action such as 'Save new vocabulary version' to distinguish this configuration write from social publishing.
6. Correct old navigation guidance and provide visible unsaved-state protection when selecting another line or archiving it.

## Validation

Compare inherited versus overridden research, a custom time zone, invalid range, archived line, weight error, and a vocabulary revision used by historical briefs.

## Scope and delivery

Do not create default profile rows as a side effect, change evaluation policy implicitly, or hardcode one Topic's vocabulary.

## Implementation progress — September 28, 2026

- Editorial-line guidance now reaches the actual management view. Switching line edits prompts before discarding changes; Save/Cancel are scoped to the line, and revision conflicts retain the draft with an explanation. Editorial weight fields show range errors beside the input. Exact periods now use date/time and explicit UTC-offset controls, and the time-zone field suggests common IANA values while accepting valid custom values. Invalid periods or time zones block Save.
- Remaining: make inheritance summaries and angles readable, and validate custom offsets, archive, conflict, and historical-vocabulary flows in the browser.

Apply the feature's [shared visual contract and definition of done](../features/ui-ux-consistency.md), including UXDSL, English UI copy, responsive/keyboard checks, and preservation of approvals and history. Record implementation and visual evidence before changing status. The [component audit](../ui-ux-component-audit.md) lists every affected consumer.
